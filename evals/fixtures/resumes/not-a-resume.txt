Notes on the Tuesday farmers market and the week ahead

The tomatoes at the second stall were much better than last week, so buy those again. Get two
loaves of the seeded sourdough because the small one goes stale by Thursday. They had the good
olive oil back in stock, the one in the green tin, so pick up a bottle before it disappears
again. Coffee beans are running low at home, the medium roast, not the dark one.

Remember the pharmacy closes early on Wednesday this week, so collect the prescription on
Tuesday afternoon instead. The parcel at the post office has been sitting there since Friday
and they only hold things for ten days, so that needs doing too.

Ella asked whether we are still going to the coast in August. Need to check the ferry timetable
before saying yes, because the last crossing was earlier than expected last year and we ended
up driving the long way round. Also should book the dog sitter well in advance this time.

The garden needs the hedge cutting back before it gets any taller, and the shed door still
sticks in wet weather. Neither is urgent but both have been on the list for a month.
