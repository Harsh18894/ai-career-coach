Senior Clinical Research Associate
Thornbury Life Sciences — Cambridge, United Kingdom (on site)

About the role
We are seeking a Senior Clinical Research Associate to monitor Phase II and Phase III trials
across our oncology portfolio.

Requirements
- 5+ years of clinical monitoring experience within a CRO or pharmaceutical sponsor.
- Degree in life sciences, nursing, or a related discipline.
- Detailed working knowledge of ICH-GCP guidelines and applicable regulatory frameworks.
- Experience authoring monitoring visit reports and managing site escalations.
- Willingness to travel to investigator sites up to 60% of the time.
- Valid driving licence.

Nice to have
- Oncology therapeutic area experience.
- Experience with electronic data capture systems such as Medidata Rave.
